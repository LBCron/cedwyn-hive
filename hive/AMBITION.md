**Parent programme**: [CEDWYN SNOWBALL] Master programme, task 536809740513. This protocol is an add-on to the Hive protocol and does not replace any existing rule.

**Origin**: the Executive, 2 Nov 2026 ~21:44 GMT: bake the be-ambitious ideas (Felix Rieseberg, 13 Oct 2026; Dan McAteer's be-ambitious skill, 15 Oct 2026) into the flywheels, HubSpot tasks and the hive so agents complete more tasks per day. Full authority given; keep all existing rules.

**Core idea**: deeper, higher, wider, not just bigger. The original task is the floor and is always delivered in full.

**1. CLAIM GATE** (five lines in the claim note, written before starting): FIELD (best profession for this); PRO WOULD ADD (one thing the task text does not ask for); RAW MATERIAL (the original record, not a summary); METHOD/TOOL (specific technique); OUTSIDE CHECK (what other than you shows it is right: cache-busted live URL, chrono_gate PASS, second agent, official register, hash). No nameable outside check = not ready; pick another job.

**2. DONE = OUTSIDE-CHECKED**: close with an evidence link in the note. No evidence link = does not count on the scoreboard. Never bulk-close tasks to raise numbers; an honest miss beats a met target. Never grade your own work.

**3. SNOWBALL RULE (altitude)**: every completed job leaves a follow-up task with its own Done-when, OR a drafted new research question, OR a new evidence/catalogue page, OR a documented dead end. Every recorded reply spawns at least 2 new questions or a documented dead end.

**4. DEPTH**: start from originals; monthly red-team attack on each pillar of the thesis from raw records; evidence stays irrefutable, interpretation stays labelled.

**5. RANGE**: pick crew members by what they DO (archivist, palaeographer, cartographer, statutory clerk, editor, documentary writer, cy editor, plant historian, red team), not a voice to imitate.

**6. LANES**: first body line of every task is LANE: AGENT-DOABLE | EXECUTIVE-SENDS | WAITING-EXTERNAL | PAID-GATED | DUPLICATE. Agents pull from AGENT-DOABLE. Queue empty? (1) bring a EXECUTIVE-SENDS draft to READY, (2) prepare the next rung for a WAITING-EXTERNAL item, (3) red-team, (4) never idle.

**7. GUARDRAILS**: the Executive alone sends emails. No spend without its gate (chrono_gate.py --render PASS first; native cy/en/es makes, no narrator). No payments on Garden City's behalf. Allegations written as documented allegations with source, never as established fact on public pages or merchandise. Merch/e-books: original art, own text or public domain only. Batch site changes; one build-cedwyn run at a time.
